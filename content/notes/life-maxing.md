Life comes up with lot of problems, not only mine everybody who is out there, be it a billionare or a poor guy there is always something to solve for self or the people around you. No I am not taking about the technical problems, not always. To make it better let me share my story as a fairy tail.

## Childhood.

Born in a middle class family(lower one), a hardworking father who I admire seeing him work so hard for our living, becoming a helping hand in managing household expenses at an early age (around 6th class - Yeah I was earning doing some crazy stuff those days which my father built for me and proud of). At this point no problems, earn a bit, spend a bit, Don't calculate what you are earning, go to school,Watch Ben 10 , ChotaBheem and kirmaada chill.

## College

Moved a college which had alot of restrictions - no phone, no internet, no socializing, no good food, no good water, very good hairfall, no good air it felt like living in a chernobyl and studying. We weren't complaining about anything except food(basic need!). It was not that bad, but not eatable for long. Here the problem we had to solve was to study and focuss less on food and take something out of the college to build a better life out.

## Worklife.

Did everything well at college, got a job at a excellent workplace I would say! Money sorted, food sorted, for a good living but due to bad lifestyle in the college whole health messed up, malnutritioned literally. So started going to gym spending on supplements ,eating well,fixing sleep schedules, slowly some how solved this problem as well.

During all this course I never looked on the problems with all my focus, Sometime I did but its a natural human tendency. I moved on , I solved , I tried my best and Alhamdulillah(Praise to the God) , he didn't let me down even at a single moment. I moved ambitiously in every room never looking at this bastard problems. I feel I became more productive then I was in any of the above moments of life.
