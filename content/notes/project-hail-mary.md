Project Hail Mary written by Andy Weir who he himself is a software engineer, got his passion to write books. The Martian is another work of Andy.
Science fiction which is based on real science experiments on real matter and fictional matter, Long story short, Dr.Ryland Grace a high school teacher on a mission to some other galaxy which is tens of light years far to know the nanometer alien species (they call it a Astrophage) which breeds on our sun and eating up the energy which will ruin all the living species on Earth if it continues for 26 years more. This happens with every star in the space except Tau Ceti where the mission takes him. Dead cruise members, A unknown alien Rocky(Grace names it!) which came for the same mission to know why Tau Ceti is not losing up. Story is how they both solve the problem to save their home worlds.

## What did this book teach me?

Generally we go through a lot of question in mind on daily basis, no I will go the other way around, we see a lot of phenomenon happening around day by day and we never tend to question things. I'll explain this through a example how this book changed my thinking or problem solving abiliity.

Example-1: In our room we had a glass bottle, I use that bottle to generally cool water faster(Glasses tend to cool faster than plastic!), Using it for months one day I kept it over an hour in the defridgerator, the glass broke down in to pieces, I just questioned myself why the glass broke up started thinking theories with little bit knowledge I have and came to a conclusion that when glasses cool down particles in the glass strink and water expands in lower temperatures.
To verify this theory I searched the internet and got the same answer.

So I was in the middle of the book when this happened to my astonishment, I never think like that or I never question things that happens randomly in life(which is death of learning ofcourse).

Example-2: Got to know I have lactose intolerance where your body spends a lot of energy to digest milk, which causes brain fog and all, but I love tea , Started searching for solutions and got to know that Lactose free milk exists. Ordered it and the taste is better than the normal milk.

Now started thinking how this could have been made and bringing theories to it, I remember reading in school there is this enzyme called Lactase which helps in breaking down lactose in body as a result produces glucose. Thought that companies figured out making lactase using chemical formulas and used it to breakdown milk to sell it lactose free and I was right!!!

## What I am trying to say?

Sometime answers exist in our old knowledge and sometimes we need to do little bit research to understand things, but first we need to question phenomenons happening around for inevitable learning.
The way the author solves each problem in the book follows this pattern, iterative thinking, testing and verifying results. And this thinking is very clear. Each theory he comes up with, the way he build things to test it up is fabulous...

Now whenever some problem arrives, be it technical or be it related to science , I first think, test out theories(if possible) and verify results. Project Hail Mary trained my brain I guess from above examples or I got trained by it, anyhow good thing happened!
