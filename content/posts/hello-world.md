## Why a public site?

Private notes rot. I've kept countless notebooks, Notion pages, and Obsidian vaults that I never revisit. Writing for a public audience forces me to clarify my thinking and produce something worth keeping.

This site is split into two areas:

- **Blog** (`/posts`) — long-form writing on software, systems, and ideas
- **Notes** (`/notes`) — shorter, rougher captures: quick references, reading notes, half-baked ideas

## What to expect

I'll write about things I'm actively working on or learning. No content calendar, no SEO strategy — just honest notes from the trenches.

Topics I expect to cover:

- Distributed systems and databases
- Developer tooling and productivity
- AI/ML engineering in production
- Book summaries and paper breakdowns

That's it for the first post. Next up: a proper deep-dive on something technical.
